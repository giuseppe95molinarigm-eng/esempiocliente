Design notes

The cover

The cover is built from the book’s own material rather than stock imagery. Two mugs stand on a low shelf, handles turned the same way, in the slanted light of a kitchen window: details taken from the first chapter. There are no hospital images or symbols of transformation. The story is about returning to ordinary life, so the cover shows ordinary life, closely observed.

Type and color

The title is set in Cormorant Garamond, a display serif with long, calm strokes; the text is EB Garamond, a classic book face made for reading at length. The palette is limited to warm oat, window light, ink and one muted blue, the narrator’s chipped mug, which returns inside the book as the only accent.

The interior

Pages are 6 × 9 inches with mirrored margins and a deeper foot. Text is justified on a 15-point baseline with controlled hyphenation, and pages are balanced to avoid widows and orphans. Running heads carry the book title on left-hand pages and the chapter title on right-hand pages, and are dropped on display pages. Chapters open low on the page with small capitals and a reduced shelf motif, so the reader enters each one quietly, as the story does.
